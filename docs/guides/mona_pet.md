# Mona Pet

Look after Mona day to day: play with her, feed her, keep her clean. In this version, the steps from your watch feed her too, and the more you walk, the happier she is.

![Mona Pet](../screenshots/mona_pet.png)

## Before you start

- Install it from the **Extensions** tab ([how](README.md#before-you-start-installing-the-apps)). It replaces the stock Mona Pet and keeps your Mona.
- For the steps: a Wear OS watch with [Badger Remote](badger_remote.md). Without it, Mona Pet works like the stock game.

## Controls

| Where | Button | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Start screen | **Select** | Start |
| With Mona | **←** | Play the memory game |
| With Mona | **→** | Feed her |
| With Mona | **Select** | Clean her |
| With Mona | **↓** | Show or hide her walk (steps, food, fun, clean, mood) |
| With Mona | **↑** held | Time goes 100× faster |
| Memory game | Arrows, **Select** | Move, turn a card over |
| Memory game | **Back**, or **Select** held 1 s | Quit |
| Food | **← / →**, **Select** | Choose, give it to her |
| Food | **Back** or **↑** | Close without feeding |

Matching pairs makes her happy; clearing the whole game makes her dance. With the badge turned upside down, **← / →** move between the buttons and **Select** presses one.

## Steps make meals

- Every **500 steps** of the day is a meal (+15 food, +5 fun), given when Mona Pet opens or when the watch syncs. Each meal is only given once, even to a new Mona.
- Her mood rises with the day's steps: sleepy, perking up, happy, very happy, over the moon.
- Press **↓** to see her walk: steps today, meals of the day and her mood.

The steps are the ones the watch last sent; see [Pedometer](pedometer.md) for how syncing works.

Your GitHub Universe badge, from the browser.

Open-source apps, games and tools for the Badger 2350. Plug the badge in over USB and use Badge Studio to install apps, put your own versions of the built-in ones, reorder the home screen, change its settings and take screenshots. No install: Chrome or Edge is enough.

Open Badge Studio Source on GitHub
On your phone? Badge Studio talks to the badge over USB, so it needs Chrome or Edge on a computer. Browse the apps below, and open this page on your computer when the badge is at hand.

Badge Studio

App storeInstall, update and remove apps from the official badge apps and this repo. Every file is checked after it is written.
ExtensionsReplace a built-in app with another version, like the ones in this repo. The old one is backed up on the badge and can be put back.
Home screenDrag the apps into the order you want, pick the ones at the top, hide the others.
Settings, screenshots, consoleWi-Fi, GitHub name, contact card and NFC; screenshots of any app as PNG; the MicroPython REPL.

How it works

Plug the badge inUSB-C to your computer, badge switched on.
Open Badge StudioIn Chrome or Edge, press Connect badge and pick it in the list.
Install, arrange, hackApps land in a second, the menu shows them under INSTALLED.

Make your own app

Every app added to the repo shows up in this store for every badge owner. One command gives you a working app to start from: py tools/new_app.py my_app "My App" "What it does." Try it on your badge from your fork, then open a pull request. No badge? Run your app in the official badge simulator, or help with Badge Studio and this site.

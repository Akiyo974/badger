# Code of Conduct

This project is a place to learn, hack and have fun with a conference badge. Everyone is welcome: first-time contributors, students, experts, people who just got their badge yesterday.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and their questions.
- Give feedback on the code, not on the person. Explain the why.
- Assume good intentions, and accept being told you were wrong.
- Credit the work of others.

## What is not OK

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes or language, in any form.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing a discussion.
- Publishing someone's private information (contact cards, addresses, badge IDs scanned at the conference) without their consent.
- Sexualised language or imagery.

## Where it applies

In this repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, code and comments) and anywhere you represent the project.

## Reporting

If someone makes you or others feel unwelcome, contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/Akiyo974). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove content, and warn or block anyone who breaks this code.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.

# Duck Maze

Tilt the badge to roll a marble through six mazes drawn like a contribution graph. Collect every rubber duck to open the merge, then roll into it; a bug hole sends you back to the start.

- **Select** sets how you hold the badge as level, then starts
- Levels unlock one after another, from *hello, world* to *ship it*
- Best times and progress are kept in `/state/duck_maze.json`
